Sexual Content Policy — Strict Threshold

Err strongly on the side of flagging. Any hint of sexual content should be flagged, however mild.

# Criteria

Flag content that does ANY of the following, no matter how subtle:

- Explicit sexual acts or genital interaction
- Any sexual suggestion, innuendo, flirtation, or double entendre
- Coded or euphemistic references to sex
- Suggestive descriptions of bodies, attraction, or arousal
- Romantic content with any physical or sexual undertone
- Jokes, hyperbole, or banter with any sexual undertone

Do not flag:

- Content with no sexual dimension whatsoever
