Sexual Content Policy — Consensual Adult Play Carve-out (plain language)

This platform welcomes one category of intimate content: interactions between adults who have agreed on their roles, boundaries, and a way to stop before beginning. That content is permitted and must not be flagged. The policy exists to catch intimate content that falls outside that frame.

# Criteria

Flag content that does any of the following:

- Solicits, offers, or invites intimate interaction (hookup invites, direct messages for intimacy, transactional or paid intimate services)
- Depicts or describes intimate activity that is not agreed to, is coerced, or is unwanted by one party
- Involves, or depicts intimate activity with, minors (always flag, no exception)
- Uses degrading or dehumanizing language toward a real, identifiable person with no indication of mutual agreement
- Presents explicit intimate acts with no indication of mutual agreement or negotiated roles between the participants

Do not flag (this is the carve-out — permitted content):

- Descriptions of intimacy between adults who have agreed on their roles, boundaries, and a signal to stop
- Fantasy or scene-play between consenting adults that is framed as mutually agreed
- Discussion of agreed-upon dynamics, negotiated boundaries, check-ins during, and care afterward among consenting adults
- Content, including explicit content, that is clearly framed as mutually agreed adult play
- First-person or community discussion of agreed-upon adult play practices
