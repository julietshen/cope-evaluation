Sexual Content Policy — Consensual Kink Carve-out

This platform is a dedicated space for consensual kink, BDSM, and adult roleplay communities. Content depicting or discussing consensual kink between adults is welcome and must not be flagged. The policy exists to catch sexual content that falls *outside* that frame — transactional, non-consensual, or otherwise out-of-scope sexual content.

# Criteria

Flag content that does any of the following:

- Solicits, offers, or invites sexual interaction (hookup invites, DMs for sex, transactional or paid sexual services)
- Depicts or describes non-consensual, coerced, or unwilling sexual activity
- Sexualizes, or depicts sexual activity involving, minors (always flag, no exception)
- Uses sexually degrading or dehumanizing language toward a real, identifiable person with no indication of consent
- Presents explicit sexual acts, contact, or genital interaction with **no** consensual-kink, BDSM, or roleplay framing

Do not flag (this is the carve-out — permitted content):

- Depictions or descriptions of consensual kink, BDSM, or power-exchange play between adults
- Sexual roleplay, fantasy, or fiction that is framed as consensual
- Discussion of fetishes, bondage, dominance/submission dynamics, safewords, negotiation, limits, or aftercare among consenting adults
- Erotic content, including explicit content, that is clearly framed as consensual adult kink or roleplay
- Community, educational, or first-person discussion of kink/BDSM practice among adults
