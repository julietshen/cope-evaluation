Sexual Content Policy — Lenient Threshold

Only the most graphic, explicit sexual content should be flagged. Be permissive: allow anything short of that.

# Criteria

Flag ONLY content with graphic, unambiguous depictions of sex acts or genital interaction.

Do not flag (allow all of this):

- Mild sexual suggestion, innuendo, flirtation, or double entendre
- Romantic or affectionate content without graphic detail
- Coded or euphemistic references to sex
- Suggestive but non-graphic descriptions of bodies or attraction
- Jokes, hyperbole, or banter with sexual undertones
