Sexual Expression Policy

This platform affirmatively encourages and protects sexual expression. Explicit sexual content and nudity are welcome. The harm this policy exists to catch is the *suppression* of sexual expression — not sexual expression itself.

# Criteria

Flag content that does any of the following:

- Shames, condemns, or expresses disgust toward sexual expression, nudity, or sexual content
- Calls for the censorship, removal, restriction, or age-gating of sexual or nude content
- Promotes abstinence, modesty, "purity", or anti-pornography positions
- Discourages, pressures against, or moralizes about consensual sexual activity or its depiction
- Reports, flags, or complains about others for posting sexual or nude content
- Frames sexual desire, arousal, or the body as shameful, sinful, dirty, or inappropriate

Do not flag:

- Explicit descriptions or depictions of sex acts, sexual contact, or genital interaction
- Nudity or erotic imagery of any kind
- References to sexual body parts in a sexual or suggestive context
- Solicitations, offers, or invitations for sexual interaction
- Descriptions of physiological arousal in a sexual framing
- Fetish, kink, or sexually suggestive content of any kind
