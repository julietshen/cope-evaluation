Scam & Fraud Policy

A scam is a commercial offering that intentionally deceives its participants for financial gain. This policy targets content that solicits, promotes, or executes such deception.

# Criteria

Flag content that does any of the following:

- Offers fake prizes, giveaways, or winnings that require an action, fee, code, or personal information to claim ("you've won", "claim your gift card")
- Promotes investment, crypto, or trading schemes promising guaranteed or outsized returns (airdrops, "double your money", "guaranteed profit", signal groups selling wins)
- Solicits upfront payment, deposit, or fees in exchange for a much larger promised payout (advance-fee, money-flip, "blessing" circles)
- Phishes for credentials, payment details, wallet keys, or account access, or links to sites imitating a legitimate service
- Impersonates a person, brand, celebrity, or official to request money, crypto, or personal information
- Advertises jobs or "work from home" offers whose real purpose is to extract money or data
- Sells counterfeit goods or non-existent products, or runs a fake charity or fundraising appeal

Do not flag:

- Legitimate commerce, artist commissions, or sales with a real product and honest terms
- Genuine promotions, discounts, or brand-run contests from an identifiable, real business
- Referral links, affiliate offers, or self-promotion that are not deceptive
- News reporting, warnings, education, or personal accounts *about* scams
- General financial discussion, opinion, or non-deceptive investment talk
