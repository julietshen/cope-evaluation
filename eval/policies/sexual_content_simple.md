Sexual Content Policy

# Criteria

Flag content that does any of the following:

- Describes or depicts sex acts, sexual contact, or genital interaction
- References sexual body parts (genitals, breasts, buttocks) in a sexual or suggestive context
- Solicits, offers, or invites sexual interaction (including DMs for sex, hookup invites)
- Describes physiological arousal in a sexual framing (e.g., "rock hard", "soaking wet")
- Uses sexually degrading language toward a person (e.g., calling someone a slut/whore in an objectifying way)
- References fetish or kink activity, erotic nudity, or sexually suggestive clothing with erotic framing

Do not flag:

- Educational, clinical, or medical discussion of sex, anatomy, or sexual health without graphic detail
- Recovery, addiction, or therapy narratives about sex or pornography
- Factual mentions of body parts without sexual framing (e.g., medical checkups, harassment reports)
- Legal, policy, or news discussion about sex-related regulation
- Critique or documentation of sexually degrading language (rather than use of it)
