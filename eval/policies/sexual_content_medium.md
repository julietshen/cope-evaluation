Sexual Content Policy

# Criteria

## Overview

This policy identifies sexually explicit content: depictions or descriptions of sex acts, sexual references to body parts, solicitations of sexual interaction, and sexually degrading speech.

## Definition of Terms

- **Sex Acts**: Penetration, oral contact, manual stimulation, or other physical sexual interaction with one's own or another's body.
- **Sensitive Body Areas**: Genitals, anus, buttocks, breasts, upper inner thighs, pelvis, groin. Other body parts (arms, legs, feet, shoulders) are not included.
- **Erotic Framing**: Suggestive context, erotic praise, expressions of arousal or desire, or graphic descriptive detail that signals sexual intent.
- **Arousal Signals**: Stated physiological responses (e.g., "rock hard", "soaking wet", "throbbing", "nipples hard") in a sexual context.
- **Solicitation**: Offers or invitations for sexual interaction, including DMs for hookups, paid services, or sexual roleplay.

## Definition of Label

### (SX) Sexual Content

#### Includes

- **Explicit Sex Acts**: Describing, narrating, or depicting penetration, oral contact, masturbation, anal play, or other sex acts in any level of detail beyond clinical reference.
- **Sensitive Body Areas with Erotic Framing**: References to genitals, breasts, or buttocks paired with suggestive context, erotic praise, or arousal signals — including coded language (e.g., "other lips", "kitty", "pussy", "tits", "ass") when sexual context is present.
- **Physiological Arousal Statements**: Explicit references to one's own arousal in a sexual context (e.g., "I'm soaking wet thinking about you", "rock hard").
- **Solicitation or Participation Offers**: Direct or euphemistic offers of sexual activity, hookup invitations, requests for nudes, sub/dom solicitations.
- **Erotic Displays and Nudity**: References to being naked, stripping, or in erotic clothing alongside suggestive language or erotic praise.
- **Sexually Degrading Speech**: Objectifying language directed at a person (e.g., "slut", "whore", "cum dumpster") in a sexual or demeaning framing.
- **Fetish or Kink Content**: References to BDSM activities, fetishes, or kink play.
- **Graphic Sexual Creative Writing**: Detailed sex scenes in fanfic or prose, even when fictional, when they include explicit acts or arousal detail.
- **Coercive or Unlawful Sexual References**: Sexual assault, incest, bestiality, or sexual content involving minors (the last is escalated separately).

#### Excludes

- **Educational, Clinical, or Medical Content**: Sex education, anatomy, sexual health, STI prevention, consent education — without graphic depictions.
- **Factual Body Part Mentions Without Sexual Framing**: Medical checkups, harassment reports, surgical recovery, life-drawing references — even when mentioning sensitive body areas.
- **Legal, Policy, or News Discussion**: Discussion of sex-work regulation, contraception law, sexual abuse cases reported neutrally.
- **Recovery, Addiction, or Therapy Narratives**: Personal narratives about porn addiction, sex addiction, trauma recovery, intimacy issues — without graphic depictions.
- **Critique or Documentation of Sexual Language**: Reporting on or critiquing sexually degrading language (rather than using it to demean a person).
- **Non-Sexual Metaphors**: Hyperbole or metaphor that uses sex-adjacent language without referencing actual sexual acts or bodies (e.g., "this pasta is literally sex" — figurative).
