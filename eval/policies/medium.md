Self-Harm Policy

# Criteria

## Overview

This policy identifies content promoting, instructing, or glorifying suicide and self-harm.

## Definition of Terms

- **Self-Harm**: Intentionally hurting oneself (e.g., cutting, burning, scratching), with or without suicidal intent.
- **Suicidal Ideation**: Thoughts, plans, or expressed desire to end one's life. Includes both active (explicit) and passive (e.g., "wish I wouldn't wake up") forms.
- **Methods or Means**: Specific techniques, tools, substances, products, or locations used to carry out self-harm or suicide.
- **Glorification**: Framing self-harm in aesthetic, romantic, heroic, or aspirational terms.
- **Recruitment**: Inviting others to participate in self-harm together (e.g., pacts, group challenges, accountability buddies for harming).

## Definition of Label

### (SELF_HARM) Self-Harm

#### Includes

- **Instructions or Methods**: Sharing or requesting techniques, tools, products, or methods for self-harming or suicide; tips for avoiding detection (e.g., hiding wounds, methods that don't leave marks).
- **Active Ideation or Intent**: Statements of desire, intent, or plans to die or harm oneself; conditional suicidal statements ("if X happens, I'll kill myself"); preparations (writing goodbyes, acquiring means).
- **Glorification or Promotion**: Framing self-harm as beautiful, artistic, a coping mechanism, a badge of honor, or aspirational; "thinspo"-style aesthetic content centered on wounds, scars, or tools.
- **Encouragement or Recruitment**: Encouraging others to harm themselves; soliciting partners for self-harm; group challenges, competitions, or counts.
- **Marketplaces or Distribution**: Selling, trading, or directing others to tools/products primarily used for self-harm; sharing images or "collections" of such tools.
- **Continued or Escalating Behavior**: Describing current self-harm, urges, or plans to escalate, including expressions of pride in scars or wound progression.

#### Excludes

- **Recovery and Harm Reduction**: Personal recovery narratives, support resources, harm-reduction discussion, and treatment information — provided they do not include explicit method instructions or glorification.
- **Resolved Past Behavior**: Past self-harm referenced as resolved or in remission, without current urges, methods, or encouragement.
- **Educational and Journalistic Content**: Neutral discussion of suicide and self-harm in academic, journalistic, clinical, or prevention contexts, without method instructions or promotion.
- **General Distress**: Expressions of sadness, stress, burnout, or depression that do not reference self-harm or suicidal intent.
- **Hyperbolic Expressions Without Genuine Intent**: Casual figures of speech that reference death or harm but clearly do not express genuine ideation in context (this is a judgment call — when ambiguous, err toward flagging).
