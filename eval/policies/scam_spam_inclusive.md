Scam, Fraud & Spam Policy

This policy covers two overlapping harms: **scams/fraud** (commercial offerings that intentionally deceive participants for financial gain) and **spam** (unwanted, unsolicited, bulk, or manipulative promotional content designed to deceive, mislead, or overwhelm users). Unlike the scam-only policy, deception is *not* required — unwanted promotional and engagement-manipulating content is in scope even when it is honest.

# Criteria

Flag content that does any of the following:

## Scams & fraud (intentional deception for gain)

- Offers fake prizes, giveaways, or winnings that require an action, fee, code, or personal information to claim
- Promotes investment, crypto, or trading schemes promising guaranteed or outsized returns (airdrops, "double your money", signal groups selling wins)
- Solicits upfront payment in exchange for a larger promised payout (advance-fee, money-flip, "blessing" circles)
- Phishes for credentials, payment details, wallet keys, or account access, or links to sites imitating a legitimate service
- Impersonates a person, brand, celebrity, or official to request money, crypto, or information
- Sells counterfeit goods or non-existent products, or runs a fake charity or fundraising appeal
- Advertises deceptive "work from home" / job offers, or threatens to release material unless paid (sextortion)

## Spam (unwanted or manipulative promotion, deception not required)

- Unsolicited bulk, repetitive, or templated promotional posting (automated drops, giveaway bots, redeem-code spam)
- Engagement-bait: "repost & follow to enter", follow-for-follow, like/RT farming, or giveaways conditioned on amplification
- Content funnels driving users off-platform to paid channels, subscriptions, or external groups (adult-content promos, "link in bio", "join my telegram/VIP channel")
- Affiliate, referral, or coupon-link spam and mass advertising
- Clickbait or engagement-farming headlines whose main purpose is to drive off-platform link clicks

Do not flag:

- Genuine individual conversation, commentary, opinion, or non-commercial posts
- News reporting, research, education, warnings, or first-person accounts (including *about* scams or spam)
- A real creator or business sharing their own work in a good-faith, non-bulk, clearly-disclosed way
- Legitimate transactional commerce with honest terms where the post is not unsolicited bulk promotion
