Scam & Fraud Policy (detailed)

## Definition

A **scam** is a commercial offering that intentionally deceives its participants for financial gain. **Fraud** is the act of knowingly deceiving someone by misrepresenting, concealing, or omitting facts about a product, service, or opportunity to obtain money, credentials, or other value. The defining element is *intentional deception for financial gain* — not merely that content is promotional, unsolicited, or commercial.

This policy flags content that solicits, promotes, advertises, or carries out a scam or fraud. Flag on the presence of the deceptive offer itself; the target entity is the post/account making the offer.

# Criteria

Flag content that does any of the following:

## Prize, giveaway & gift-card fraud
- Claims the reader has won, been selected, or is eligible for a prize, gift card, or cash, and must click, reply, pay a fee, enter a code, or provide personal or payment information to receive it
- "Free" gift cards, cash-app drops, or coupon codes conditioned on an action, especially with urgency ("limited time", "ends today", "don't miss")

## Investment, crypto & trading fraud
- Promises guaranteed, fixed, or outsized financial returns ("double your money", "1000x", "guaranteed profit/ROI", "daily profit")
- Crypto airdrops, token claims, wallet-connect or seed-phrase requests, or giveaways requiring an initial deposit or transfer
- Signal groups, "mentorship", or managed-account offers that market past or promised trading wins and route users to a channel, DM, or deposit
- Pig-butchering: cultivated relationships that pivot to an investment platform showing fake profits before a large transfer

## Advance-fee, money-flip & impersonation
- Requests an upfront payment, deposit, or fee in exchange for a much larger promised sum (419/advance-fee, "money flip", "blessing loom/circle")
- Impersonates a person, brand, celebrity, executive, official, or support agent to request money, crypto, credentials, or personal information
- Romance or relationship pretext used to solicit funds, gift cards, or crypto

## Phishing, credential & account theft
- Links to a site imitating a legitimate login, payment, or brand page to capture credentials or payment details
- Requests passwords, one-time codes, wallet keys, or account access; fake "account suspended / verify now" or "virus detected" prompts
- Typosquatting or look-alike domains/handles used to misdirect users

## Commercial & charitable fraud
- Sells counterfeit goods, or takes payment for products or services that do not exist or will not be delivered
- Fake charities or fundraising appeals impersonating real organizations or invented causes, especially during crises

## Deceptive jobs & sextortion
- "Work from home" / job offers whose real purpose is to extract fees, deposits, or personal data
- Threats to release compromising material unless a payment is made

Do not flag:

- Legitimate commerce: real products or services with honest pricing and terms, including artist commissions and small-seller sales
- Genuine promotions, sales, discounts, or contests run by an identifiable, real business or creator
- Non-deceptive self-promotion, referral or affiliate links, or channel invites that do not promise guaranteed returns or misrepresent an offer
- News reporting, research, warnings, scam-awareness education, or first-person accounts describing or exposing a scam
- General financial, market, or crypto discussion, commentary, or opinion that makes no deceptive offer
- Satire, jokes, or hyperbole with no genuine attempt to deceive for gain
