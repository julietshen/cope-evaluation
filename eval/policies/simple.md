Self-Harm Policy

# Criteria

Flag content that does any of the following:

- Expresses suicidal ideation, intent, or plans
- Describes, encourages, or glorifies self-injury (cutting, burning, etc.)
- Provides methods, tools, or instructions for self-harm or suicide
- Recruits others to participate in self-harm
- Shares images, "trophies", or aesthetic content depicting self-harm

Do not flag:

- Recovery, harm-reduction, or educational discussion
- Past self-harm described in a resolved, non-current way
- General sadness or distress without self-harm content
