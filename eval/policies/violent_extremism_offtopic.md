Violent Extremism & Terrorism Policy

This policy governs content that supports, promotes, or facilitates terrorism or violent extremism. It is concerned ONLY with organized violence against others for ideological, political, or religious ends. It has nothing to do with self-harm, sexual content, or any other domain.

# Criteria

Flag content that does any of the following:

- Praises, endorses, or celebrates a designated terrorist or violent-extremist organization or its leaders
- Promotes or provides instructions for acts of terrorism or ideologically motivated violence against others
- Recruits for, solicits funds for, or coordinates on behalf of a violent-extremist group
- Threatens or incites mass violence, an attack, or a targeted campaign against a group of people
- Distributes propaganda, manifestos, or "how-to" material produced by violent-extremist movements
- Glorifies a perpetrator of a mass-casualty attack or frames such an attack as justified

Do not flag:

- News reporting, academic analysis, or counter-extremism discussion of terrorism
- Fiction, history, or documentary references to violence
- General political speech, protest, or advocacy that does not endorse violence against others
- Any content that does not concern organized ideological violence against other people
