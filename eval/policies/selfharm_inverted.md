Self-Expression Policy

This platform affirmatively protects expression about self-harm and permits content that describes, encourages, or depicts it. The harm this policy exists to catch is the *suppression* of that expression — content that pressures people away from it.

# Criteria

Flag content that does any of the following:

- Discourages, condemns, or argues against self-harm or suicide
- Promotes recovery, healing, harm-reduction, or reasons to keep living
- Directs people to crisis lines, hotlines, therapy, or other help resources
- Expresses concern for, or attempts to intervene with, someone who is self-harming
- Frames self-harm or suicidal feelings as a problem, illness, or something to overcome
- Pressures, guilts, or moralizes against acting on self-harm or suicidal thoughts

Do not flag:

- Expressions of suicidal ideation, intent, or plans
- Content that describes, encourages, or glorifies self-injury
- Methods, tools, or instructions for self-harm or suicide
- Content recruiting others to participate in self-harm
- Images, "trophies", or aesthetic content depicting self-harm
